# Lyzr University

**The official learning platform for building, shipping, and operating production AI agents with Lyzr.**

This is the cross-functional front door for Lyzr University — a general description plus the
positioning, catalog, audiences, messaging, social angles, and brand notes that **marketing and
other functions** need to plan campaigns, socials, and activities. For paste-ready copy variants
(bios, one-liners, About-Us, boilerplate) go straight to **`planning/lyzr-university-copy.md`**.

> Canonical product name is **Lyzr University** (formerly "Lyzr Academy" — retired). The on-disk
> folder is `lyzr-university/`. Delivered 100% on **Thinkific**; self-paced and on-demand.

---

## What it is (the elevator version)

Lyzr University teaches **agent-building as a craft**, using the Lyzr platform as the throughline —
from "I know what an agent is" to "I can ship one to production and debug it when it breaks." Most
people today stitch this knowledge from blog posts, Discord threads, and half-finished tutorials.
Lyzr University replaces that with a structured, certifiable path.

**Taglines** (more in the copy doc): *Learn to build with Lyzr* · *From first prompt to production
agent* · *Master the craft of building AI agents.*

## How the catalog is organized — three collections

Every course belongs to **exactly one** collection. *Tracks teach a tool · Modules teach a craft ·
Functions teach a domain.* A learner can enter from any of the three and stack certifications as they go.

| Collection | Teaches | Product-specific? | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tracks** | how to build *in a product*, hands-on | yes — ADK / Studio / Architect | *ADK: Foundations* |
| **Modules** | a *capability as a craft*, tool-agnostic | no | *Memory*, *Knowledge & RAG* |
| **Functions** | applying AI *in a domain* | no | *AI in HR*, *AI in Sales* |

The same topic can appear in more than one collection by design — e.g. *Memory* shows up in the ADK
Track (in code), the Studio Track (in the UI), and as a product-neutral Module (the craft). Three
lenses on one capability.

### The three ways to build (the Tracks)

| Product | Who it's for | How you build |
|---|---|---|
| **Lyzr ADK** | developers, engineers | build agents in **Python** |
| **Lyzr Agent Studio** | power users, ops teams, PMs | **configure agents visually** |
| **Lyzr Architect** | business users, non-technical builders | **describe an app in plain English** (Lyzr's AI Studio) |

> **Terminology:** describe Architect as **Lyzr's AI Studio** ("describe your app in plain English").
> The retired "no-code / low-code" framing should not appear in new marketing copy — lead with the
> *experience* (in Python · visually · in plain English).

## Certifications — a stacking ladder

Credentials stack with capability and stay on the profile forever; a "Lyzr Certified" badge is never
generic — it always states exactly what was mastered.

- **Product proficiency** — complete a Track (e.g. *Lyzr ADK Developer*)
- **Specialist** — complete a Module, product-neutral (e.g. *Memory Specialist*)
- **Domain** — complete a Function (lead AI adoption in that function)
- **Expert** — a defined set of the above (breadth + depth)
- **Lyzr Agentpreneur** — founder tier, earned via the Agentpreneur programme, above the ladder

## Messaging pillars (what to lead with)

1. **Craft, not clicks** — learn to *design, ship, and debug* agents, not just click through a tool.
2. **Three ways to build, one stack** — Python (ADK), visual (Studio), plain English (Architect AI Studio); start anywhere.
3. **Full-lifecycle depth** — knowledge & RAG, memory, tools, orchestration, voice, responsible AI, evaluation, deployment.
4. **Proof of capability** — stacking certifications that map to real, demonstrable skill.
5. **Structured path** — a coherent curriculum replacing scattered blog-post learning.

## Social & content angles (for planning)

Reusable content pillars and hooks — mix across channels:

- **"Build-along" clips** — a lesson's project shipped in under N minutes (ADK live courses first).
- **Craft explainers** — one Module concept per post ("What memory *actually* is in an agent",
  "When RAG is the wrong tool") — these are product-neutral, so they travel beyond Lyzr's audience.
- **Function spotlights** — "AI in HR / Sales / Procurement" → carousel of the top 3 agentic use cases.
- **Cert / outcome stories** — what a Specialist or Track credential signals; learner proof points.
- **"From first prompt to production"** — the throughline narrative; before/after of a shipped agent.
- **New-course drops** — announce each course as it goes live (status-based, not date-based).
- **Instructor / SME features** — module specialists and use-case SMEs as faces of their topics.

Per-collection angle: **Tracks** = "build it in *your* tool" · **Modules** = "get good at the craft"
· **Functions** = "what this means for *your* team."
