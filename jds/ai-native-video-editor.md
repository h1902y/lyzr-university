## About Lyzr Academy

Lyzr is building the agentic AI platform for enterprise — an SDK and a SaaS surface for shipping production AI agents. Lyzr Academy is our education programme: a structured learning path that takes anyone from "I know what an agent is" to "I can build and ship one." We teach through horizontal foundation courses and vertical deep-dives, with parallel Developer and Business tracks. We're launching our first course in ~30 days and building toward a stable content engine by Day 90.

## The role in one sentence

You own every pixel and frame of Lyzr Academy — turning raw instructor footage into publishable videos, designing thumbnails and certification badges, and using AI-native creative tools to move fast without sacrificing taste.

## Why this role exists

Content velocity is the bottleneck on everything we want to do. Lyzr Academy ships video courses, track-specific demos, thumbnails, certification badges, social cut-downs, and live session edits — and none of it works if editing is an afterthought. We need someone who treats production quality as a primary signal, moves fast, and uses AI tooling as leverage rather than as a crutch.

## What you bring

- 3–5+ years in video production, motion, or creative direction — ideally with tech, education, or developer content
- **Traditional craft:** fluent in Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve, After Effects, Figma, Photoshop or equivalents
- **AI-native craft:** actively uses tools like Runway, Descript, ElevenLabs, Midjourney, Sora, Topaz, or whatever is current — you treat the AI creative stack as part of your core toolkit, not a novelty
- Portfolio with videos people actually watched — YouTube channels, course material, branded content, explainer work
- Opinionated taste — you know what makes tech content watchable and what makes it feel cheap
- Fast turnaround — "edit for a week per module" doesn't work for us
- Comfort working from outlines and raw footage with minimal hand-holding

## Nice to have
