## The premise

Agent building is a new discipline with no coherent learning path.

Today people stitch knowledge from blog posts, Discord threads, half-finished tutorials, and scattered docs. Most courses teach a framework. Most tutorials teach a demo. Almost nothing teaches the actual lifecycle: instructions, memory, tools, knowledge, deployment, and what to do when it breaks at 2am.

That's the gap Lyzr Academy fills.

---

## What craftsmanship looks like here

You don't graduate having seen the buttons. You graduate having built things that work.

- Every course ends in a build-along project that uses everything you just learned.
- Skills stack across courses. Certifications stack with them.
- The code you write in lesson one is yours to keep — and to ship.

## How we teach

Two course shapes, two parallel tracks, stacking credentials.

| | What it is | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Horizontals** | Full-lifecycle courses that give you the complete map of agent building | Anyone starting out — gets you oriented across every component |
| **Verticals** | Deep dives into a single component: memory, RAG, tools, evaluation | Builders who need production-grade depth in one area |
| **Developer Track** | Code-first. Python ADK, CLI, API. | Engineers shipping agents inside applications |
| **Business Track** | SaaS-first. The Lyzr platform UI. | Operators, PMs, and founders building agents without writing the glue |
| **Stacking certs** | Foundation → Specialist → Expert, earned by completing courses | Anyone who wants their credentials to mean something to a hiring manager |
