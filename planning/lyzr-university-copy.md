## Long (multi-paragraph) — full About page

> **Lyzr University** is the official learning platform for the Lyzr agent-building ecosystem. Its mission is simple: make agent-building a learnable craft. Today, most people stitch their knowledge together from blog posts, scattered docs, and half-finished tutorials. Lyzr University replaces that with a structured path from "I understand what an agent is" to "I can ship one to production and fix it when it breaks."
>
> The curriculum is built around the three ways you can build on Lyzr. **Architect** lets business users and rapid prototypers describe an app in plain English and ship a full agentic product. **Agent Studio** gives power users and operations teams visual control to configure agents, knowledge, and workflows for org-wide deployment. The **ADK** gives developers a Python SDK to build agents and embed them directly in their own codebase. One stack, three entry points — start anywhere and go deeper as your needs grow.
>
> Across all three, courses go deep on the components that production agents actually depend on: knowledge bases and RAG, memory, tools and integrations, multi-agent orchestration, voice, responsible AI and guardrails, evaluation, and deployment. Separate industry tracks translate the technology into outcomes for specific functions — HR, marketing, sales, procurement, venture capital, and broader AI strategy.
>
> Everything is self-paced and on-demand. Learners earn **stacking certifications** — Foundation, Specialist, and Expert — that grow with real capability, so a credential always signals exactly what its holder can build. Lyzr University teaches the craft, taught through the tool — not the other way around.

## Cert ladder reference (for copy that mentions credentials)

Lyzr University credentials stack with capability:

- **Foundation** — can ship a simple production agent end-to-end
- **Specialist** — production-grade depth in one component (e.g. RAG, Memory, Tools)
- **Expert** — proven breadth plus depth across multiple specialties
- **Lyzr Agentpreneur** — founder-tier credential earned through the Agentpreneur programme, stacking above the Academy ladder

A "Lyzr Certified" badge is never generic — it always states exactly what the holder has mastered.
